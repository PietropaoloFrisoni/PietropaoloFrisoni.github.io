01

Love for science

I'm a computational scientist with a PhD in Physics and Scientific Computing. I enjoy working at the intersection of mathematics, physics, and computer science.

My journey began in theoretical physics, where I worked on quantum gravity and discovered that some of its most challenging open questions were fundamentally computational.

Tackling them leads naturally to high-performance computing and, from there, to quantum computing, where the challenge today is making quantum computers programmable and accessible.

Along the way, I've had the opportunity to live, study, and work in different countries, collaborating with people from all over the world.

You'll usually find me playing the guitar, a passion I've had since I was ten.
